NLP & Hybrid Pipeline

SmartCourse implements a dual-processing logic to ensure users find courses through both direct terminology and broader conceptual intent.

Method 01
TF-IDF Model

Classical content-based filtering. Utilizes N-Grams (unigrams and bigrams) to prioritize exact keyword matches. This ensures that specific tools (e.g., "Django") are weighted higher than general terms.

Method 02
Neural Model

Leverages the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 transformer. It converts queries into dense vectors, allowing the system to understand that a search for "web design" is semantically related to "UX/UI".
